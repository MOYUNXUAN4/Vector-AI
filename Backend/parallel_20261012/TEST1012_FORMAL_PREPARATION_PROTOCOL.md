Scope: 15 new-configuration development sessions only. No fits, probabilities, threshold selection, alarm-policy changes or merging old/frozen datasets. Preserve raw files and historical outputs. Write derived preparation results only under `parallel_20261012/test1012_formal_prepare/`.

## Locked processing rules

Reuse reviewed DEV_MATCHED_A preparation/helper source code, with explicit isolated output paths and TEST1012 association inputs. Do not execute their default entry points against old directories. Preserve existing 24 causal features, 0.05s decision stride, 0.5s history, 0.1s source gap limit and run/platform/layer/ordinary-generation segmentation. Keep raw calibration_completed outcome. Analysis-only three-layer completion requires ended, passed_count=3 and selected_session_layers=3. ## Geometry event evidence

Use adjacent valid full geometry query snapshots, actual query time, same run/platform/layer/ordinary generation, and a documented same-run clock bridge. Diagnostic fingerprint versions and ordinary geometry generations are distinct; do not require equal strings or silently substitute one for the other. Do not skip invalid intervening samples or replace query times with trigger times.

Terminal crossings require independent edge_exit predicate event, last-support ownership and terminal CSV identity/layer corroboration. Unknown or interrupted evidence remains unknown; do not fill from the earlier raw-CSV crossing proxy. Preserve lower/upper timing bounds and return/censoring status. Final game end time is a separate endpoint, not a substitute for geometric crossing.

## Primary review before any training

Verify source stability, exact row/sequence/time identity, segment history and causality; check original feature computations against the reviewed helper. Review every terminal ownership case, missing anchors and invalid intervals. Check source gap resets and layer-4/post-terminal exclusion. Preserve dataset/session/run IDs in outputs. Outcome-conditioned recovery labels may use later evidence for grouping, but prediction inputs cannot use that evidence.

Only after this preparation passes can a separate controlled deployment-model comparison or low-sensitivity refit protocol be defined. Changing sensitivity does not automatically prove the old model invalid or authorize replacing it. A new version requires new-configuration validation and remains experimental until independently accepted.
